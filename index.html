Sheer Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version: Sheer doesn't collect anything about you. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or crash reporting, and I run no servers for it. What Sheer keeps stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

Sheer is a web browser made by me, Tobias Behre, an independent developer. This policy explains what Sheer keeps on your device, what it sends to others while you use it, and how to delete your data.

What I collect

Nothing. Sheer contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it never sends me information about you or your browsing. There are no developer servers, so I can't see the sites you visit, what you search for, or anything you save.

There are two exceptions, and both are your choice:

  • Bug reports. If you send one (Settings › About Sheer › Report a Bug), I receive the email you send: your email address, what you write, and the details listed before you send it (app version, iOS version, device model, your address bar and toolbar layout, the window size, whether Private Browsing is on, the names of your extensions, memory use and how many tabs are open). The current page's address and a screenshot are included only if you switch them on. I use reports only to fix problems in Sheer, and I don't share them.
  • Apple's analytics. If you turned on Share With App Developers (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash logs and usage statistics with me. That's between you and Apple, and you can turn it off there.

What stays on your device

Sheer keeps the following in its own storage on your device. None of it is sent to me.

  • History: the pages you visit in normal tabs (address, title and visit times), for up to 90 days. Private tabs never appear in History.
  • Bookmarks, Favorites and the Reading List, including offline copies of pages you save for later.
  • Downloads you start, until you delete them. Files downloaded in private tabs are erased with the private tabs.
  • Your tabs: the open tabs and their back and forward history, so they come back after Sheer closes, and up to 20 recently closed tabs.
  • Tab thumbnails: small pictures of your tabs for the tab switcher. Private tabs' thumbnails stay in memory only.
  • Private tabs: with Keep private tabs when closing the app on (the default), private tabs, their history and their cookies are saved until you close the last private tab, and then erased. With it off, private tabs and their history and cookies are never written to disk; files you download in them are kept only until the private session ends.
  • Website data: cookies, caches and site storage, kept by WebKit, Apple's web engine, as in any browser.
  • Site settings you choose for a website, such as Desktop Site, zoom, JavaScript, or a permission answer you keep.
  • Site icons for the start page, the Library and suggestions, in a cache that iOS can clear.
  • Extensions you install, and the data they store.
  • Your settings, and a photo if you pick one for the new tab background.

Like any app's data, most of this is part of your device backups (iCloud or a computer). Tab thumbnails and site icons are not.

What is sent to others

Sheer connects to the internet only to do what you ask of it:

  • Websites. The sites you visit, and the content they load from elsewhere, receive what every browser sends: your IP address, the browser identity (Settings › Advanced), and that site's cookies. Their own privacy policies apply. Sheer can hide some of it from them through extensions and content blockers, but can't promise a site learns nothing.
  • Your search engine. A search goes to the search engine you choose (Settings › Search Engine; Google by default). While you type in the address bar, Sheer asks that engine for suggestions, in private tabs too. Those requests carry no cookies and nothing is kept. Turn off Search suggestions, or choose Websites Only (Settings › Suggestions), and nothing you type leaves your device until you go.
  • Site icons. For the start page, the Library and the address bar's suggestions, Sheer asks a site for its own icon, without cookies. It never does this in Private Browsing.
  • Pages you save. An offline copy for the Reading List, an image you save, or a preview of a tab you haven't opened yet is fetched from the site it comes from.
  • Extensions. Install uBlock Origin Lite downloads it from the project's releases on GitHub, or from the Chrome Web Store if that fails. Adding an extension from the Chrome Web Store downloads it from Google; one from a link you enter, from that address. An extension runs on the pages you allow and can make its own connections, under its developer's privacy policy.

Sheer doesn't sell, rent or share your data with anyone. It has none to share.

Permissions

Sheer asks iOS for these only when they're needed:

  • Camera and microphone: when a website asks to use them, for example for a video call. You decide for each site.
  • Location: when a website asks for it, for example a map. You decide for each site.
  • Photos (add only): to save an image you choose to your photo library. Sheer can't see your library. A new tab background photo comes through the system's photo picker, which gives Sheer only the photo you pick.

You can change these in the iOS Settings app, under Apps › Sheer. Answers you keep for a site are in that site's settings in Sheer.

Deleting your data

  • History, cookies and website data: ⋯ › Delete History…, then choose a time range. To remove single pages, swipe left in Library › History.
  • Bookmarks, Favorites and the Reading List: in the Library, swipe left on an item.
  • Downloads: Library › Downloads, or Settings › Privacy & Websites › Clear Downloads…
  • Site settings: Settings › Privacy & Websites › Websites, swipe left.
  • Private tabs: close the last private tab, and Sheer erases them with their data.
  • Extensions: Settings › Extensions, then remove the extension.
  • Everything: delete the Sheer app. iOS removes all of its data with it, apart from copies in your backups.

Children

Sheer isn't made for children in particular, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update, with a new date at the top.
